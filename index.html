Wire & Fasteners

Essential site materials.

Complementary products for reinforcement, tying, fencing, carpentry and general construction requirements.

05

Galvanized Steel Wire

Hot-dip galvanized mild steel wire with corrosion resistance for fencing, rebar tying, mesh, gabions and industrial binding.

1–25 mm diameters • 5, 25, 50 and 100 kg coils.

06

Black Annealed Binding Wire

Soft, flexible annealed wire for rebar and mesh tying, baling, bundling and wrapping.

1.5 mm & 2.5 mm • coils or pre-cut lengths.

Common Round Wire Nails

Construction nails for timber framing, formwork assembly, roofing, joinery and flooring.

25–150 mm • bright or hot-dip galvanized finish.
